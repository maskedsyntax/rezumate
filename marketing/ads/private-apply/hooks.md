# Hooks (first ~1.5s)

Scores: scroll-stop / visual / relevance / clarity / emotion / novelty.

## pain-solution
1. **Macro "SUBMIT APPLICATION" tap → resume flies off, "Applied." slams in → rejection emails stack.** 9/9/10/9/9/7 ← **HERO**
2. Inbox of "Unfortunately…" subjects scrolling too fast to read, one frozen and cropped huge. 8/8/9/8/9/6
3. "Application sent" counter ticking 1→38 with no replies. 8/7/9/8/8/6 (counter is dramatization only, no claim)
4. Cursor hovering over a resume and a job post side by side, with mismatched words lighting up red. 7/8/9/7/6/7
5. Huge type: "Did a human even read it?" typed and deleted. 7/6/9/9/8/6

## transformation
1. A score box stamps "42" in red, then smash-cuts to "84" in green. 8/9/8/7/6/7
2. The same bullet, "Worked on" dissolving into "Contributed to". 6/7/8/7/5/7
3. A yellow chip tapped, flying into the Skills line, turning green. 7/9/8/6/5/8
4. A split frame with the resume on one side and the JD on the other, zipper-merging. 6/8/7/6/5/7
5. A red score bar filling to green. 6/7/7/7/5/5

## contrarian
1. A resume with name/phone/email flying up into a cloud, then pulled back into a phone. 8/9/8/7/7/8
2. A "Create account to see your score" wall, crossed out by a physical ink line. 7/7/8/7/6/8
3. "Your resume has your home address." with the address highlighted. 8/6/8/8/8/7
4. A lock snapping around a document inside a phone. 6/7/7/7/5/5
5. "Upload?" button being pulled back off-screen. 6/6/7/6/5/7

**Hero:** pain-solution #1. **Variants (Phase 14):** pain #5 ("Did a human even read it?"), contrarian #1 (privacy-first open), transformation #1 (result-first open).

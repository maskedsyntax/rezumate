# Campaign brief

- Product: Rezumate: ATS Resume Checker (iOS)
- Campaign: private-apply
- Duration: 30s (fits the App Store preview limit and works for Reels/Shorts/TikTok)
- Format: vertical 1080×1920, 30fps
- Channel: short-form social / performance, also usable as an App Store preview base
- Forced strategy: none (chose pain-solution → transformation, with a contrarian privacy beat)
- Assumptions: no real screen recordings exist, so the UI is rebuilt from the SwiftUI source (same tokens, copy and layout) with sample resume data. Music and SFX are synthesized in-house (no licensing).

## Job of this ad

Make job seekers feel the silent-rejection loop, then show Rezumate as the private check they run before every application.

## Single-minded proposition

Know your resume fits the job before you apply, and keep it on your iPhone.

## Proof we can show

Real UI flow: upload → paste JD → score with breakdown → tap missing skill → Add to Skills → score updates → weak verb strengthened → ATS-safe PDF. Privacy is shown as the resume staying inside the phone.

## CTA

"Know before you apply." / Rezumate: private ATS resume checker / Download on the App Store · iPhone only

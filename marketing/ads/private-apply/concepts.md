# Concepts

## A. "Know before you apply" ✅ selected
The silent-rejection loop (submit → auto-rejections) → the filter question (the resume scanned against the JD, with keywords missing) → privacy pivot (personal data tempted toward a cloud, pulled back into the iPhone) → real flow in Rezumate (upload, paste, score, add a skill you have, stronger verb, PDF) → the hook's submit button returns, now tapped with a tailored resume → end card.
Metaphor: the Submit button as a gamble vs a checked move. Rank 1: strongest emotional arc, and the callback ending closes the loop.

## B. "The resume that never left"
One continuous camera follows a single resume document through the whole ad. It never leaves the phone's glass, and the camera orbits the phone while analysis happens on the document itself. It's more premium and privacy-led but weaker on the job-application pain. Rank 2, and a good privacy-variant base.

## C. "Two columns"
Split screen with the JD on the left and the resume on the right. Words from the JD hunt for matches on the resume side, and Rezumate fills the gaps honestly. Clear mechanism, but it leans on the split-screen layout and has less emotion. Rank 3.

# Audience

## Primary

Active job seekers applying to many roles in a row (new grads, career switchers, laid-off tech workers) who customize each application from their phone and suspect their resume is being filtered out before anyone reads it.

## Secondary

Privacy-conscious professionals who don't want their full contact details and work history sitting on an unknown resume-checker's server. This includes people who are job-hunting while still employed.

## Their words

- "I applied to 40 jobs and heard nothing back."
- "Did a human even read it?"
- "Every ATS checker wants me to upload my resume and make an account."
- "I don't know which keywords they want."
- "Another $30/month subscription for a resume tool?"

## Outcome they want

To hit apply knowing this version fits this job, not to hope that it does.

## Objections

- "Is my data safe?" → it runs on-device, with no account.
- "Will it make stuff up?" → it only strengthens existing wording, and you confirm every added skill.
- "Another subscription?" → free tier, plus a one-time Pro.
- "Is it on Android?" → not yet. iPhone only.

## Weak angles

Generic "AI resume builder", "land your dream job", guaranteed interviews, fake ATS rejection statistics.

## Strong angles

The silent rejection loop → the filter question → privacy → a visible fix, all before hitting apply.
